# Appendix A: Alignment with Established Practices

## A.1 Purpose

This appendix shows how the framework relates to established methods and standards. It states alignment only; it does not claim conformance to any standard.

## A.2 PDCA

Every layer follows the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle through its behaviors:

| Step | [Value](04-value-layer.md#45-behavior) | [Delivery](03-delivery-layer.md#35-behavior) | [AI](02-ai-layer.md#25-behavior) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan | [Discover](04-value-layer.md#451-discover), [Shape](04-value-layer.md#452-shape), [Prioritize](04-value-layer.md#453-prioritize) | [Explain](03-delivery-layer.md#351-explain), [Evaluate](03-delivery-layer.md#352-evaluate) | [Explore](02-ai-layer.md#251-explore), [Assess](02-ai-layer.md#254-assess) |
| Do | [Commit](04-value-layer.md#454-commit), [Realize](04-value-layer.md#455-realize) | [Deliver](03-delivery-layer.md#353-deliver) | [Change](02-ai-layer.md#252-change) |
| Check | [Measure](04-value-layer.md#456-measure) | [Validate](03-delivery-layer.md#354-validate) | [Verify](02-ai-layer.md#253-verify) |
| Act | [Steer](04-value-layer.md#457-steer) | [Confirm](03-delivery-layer.md#355-confirm) | [Reconcile](02-ai-layer.md#255-reconcile) |

- **Do in Value.** Delivery carries out the committed intent between Commit and Realize.
- **Act in Delivery and AI.** Act is shared with the next layer out: Value decides what to do with Delivery's results, and Delivery decides what to do with the AI layer's results. Confirm and Reconcile keep what is known about the product consistent with what it does, so the next Plan starts from the product as it really is. Corrections found by Reconcile go through Change.
- **Not every request runs the full cycle.** Lifecycle stage and adoption maturity decide which steps apply.

## A.3 ISO/IEC 33020

The [adoption maturity levels](05-maturity.md#54-levels) follow the process capability levels of ISO/IEC 33020: 0 Incomplete, 1 Performed, 2 Managed, 3 Established, 4 Predictable, 5 Innovating. The framework is aligned with that scale; it does not claim conformance to it.
