# Appendix B: Glossary

Terms the handbook defines in its chapters are listed with a link to their definition only. Terms it uses without defining them elsewhere are defined here.

- **Adaptation parameters:** see [2.2](02-ai-layer.md#22-contracts).
- **Adoption maturity:** see [1.5](01-layers.md#15-two-kinds-of-maturity) and [Chapter 5](05-maturity.md).
- **Area:** see [1.5](01-layers.md#15-two-kinds-of-maturity).
- **Behavior:** see [1.3](01-layers.md#13-contracts-and-behavior).
- **Boundary:** the limit of what a request may look at or change. What is noticed outside it is reported, not acted on ([1.8.1](01-layers.md#181-authority)).
- **Claim:** a statement about the product that is checked against it: about what the product does ([Reconcile](02-ai-layer.md#225-reconcile)), or about the product in business terms, such as a commitment to customers ([Confirm](03-delivery-layer.md#325-confirm)).
- **Contract:** see [1.3](01-layers.md#13-contracts-and-behavior).
- **Convention:** a craft rule of a layer, shared by all the skills of that layer ([6.3.1](06-implementation-guidelines.md#631-skill)).
- **Draft:** an output of the AI in the Delivery or Value layer. It becomes knowledge or a decision only when its owner confirms it ([3.4.5](03-delivery-layer.md#345-autonomy), [4.4.6](04-value-layer.md#446-autonomy)).
- **Element:** see [1.9](01-layers.md#19-what-defines-each-layer). In Chapter 6, the word also names the parts of a behavior's implementation ([6.2](06-implementation-guidelines.md#62-elements-of-an-implementation)).
- **Initiative:** see the outputs of [Shape (4.5.2)](04-value-layer.md#452-shape).
- **Intent:** what is wanted and why, without the solution. Value commits an intent, and Delivery delivers it. An intent not yet committed can be evaluated ([3.2](03-delivery-layer.md#32-contracts)).
- **Job description:** see [2.2.2](02-ai-layer.md#222-change).
- **Lifecycle stage:** see [1.5](01-layers.md#15-two-kinds-of-maturity).
- **Policy:** see [1.8](01-layers.md#18-framework-policies).
- **Principle:** see [1.7](01-layers.md#17-principles).
- **Product knowledge:** see [1.4](01-layers.md#14-product-knowledge-a-cross-cutting-concern).
- **Proof of change:** the evidence returned with a change that it fulfills its job description and leaves everything else intact ([2.5.2](02-ai-layer.md#252-change)).
- **Request:** any inquiry or contribution to the development of the product, made through the roles and the AI. A request may be answered from knowledge already held, and need not lead to any change ([Introduction](00-introduction.md#requests)).
- **Sensitive area:** an area of the product where work is never fully autonomous, such as authentication, authorization, secrets, or licensing ([1.8.2](01-layers.md#182-autonomy), [2.4.2](02-ai-layer.md#242-data-and-security)). Its list is kept current by the AI Enablement Lead ([9.3.2](roles/09-ai-enablement-lead.md#932-in-the-ai-layer)).
- **Skill:** the codified instructions the AI follows for a behavior ([6.3.1](06-implementation-guidelines.md#631-skill)).
- **Source of truth:** see [1.4.2](01-layers.md#142-sources-of-truth).
- **Stop:** an answer that names what is missing, unclear, or in conflict, as a question, instead of a result built on an assumption ([1.8.1](01-layers.md#181-authority)). A stop is a complete answer ([6.3.5](06-implementation-guidelines.md#635-output)).
- **Trace:** see [1.8.3](01-layers.md#183-trace).
